Accounting wants invoices in the app. Users open "Invoices" from the main navigation and see a table at `/invoices`, sorted by number, with number, customer, amount written like `$1,250.00` and a status badge: "Draft", "Sent" or "Paid". Under the table there is a line `Outstanding: $1,250.00` with the sum of the sent invoices. A "New invoice" button opens `/invoices/new`, a form with a "Create" button that brings them back to the list.

An invoice has: "Number" (`number`, the format `INV-` and four digits, message "Use the format INV-0001", unique), "Customer" (`customer`, required, up to 100 characters, message "Customer is required"), "Amount" (`amount`, a number input, greater than 0 with at most two decimals) and `status`: `draft`, `sent` or `paid`. The status is not part of the form: a new invoice is always a draft, whatever the client sends, and PATCH cannot change it either.

The status moves one step at a time through actions: `POST /api/invoices/:invoiceId/send` turns a draft into sent, `POST /api/invoices/:invoiceId/pay` turns a sent invoice into paid; both return the invoice, answer 404 for an unknown id and 409 with `{ "message": "Invoice is not in the right state" }` otherwise. In the table a draft has a button with the accessible name "Send <number>", a sent invoice has "Mark paid <number>", a paid one has no button; the badge and the outstanding total follow.

A duplicate number answers 409 with `{ "message": "An invoice with this number already exists" }`, and the form shows that message under the Number field and stays open. A paid invoice cannot be deleted: 409 with `{ "message": "A paid invoice cannot be deleted" }`.

It must be a real feature following the same layering as tasks: contract in `shared/invoices.ts`, a table with a migration and two seeded invoices (one sent, one draft), REST routes under `/api/invoices` (list, get, create, update with PATCH, delete, plus the two actions) answering 400 for invalid bodies and 404 for unknown ids, RTK Query endpoints, and tests on both sides.
